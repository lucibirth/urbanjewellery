Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Urban Store Jewelry, we want you to be happy with your purchase. If you are not completely satisfied with your jewelry, you may be eligible to return it in accordance with the conditions below.

1. Return Window

We accept eligible returns within 30 days of delivery.

To qualify for a return, the item must:

  • Be unused and unworn
  • Be in its original condition
  • Include the original packaging
  • Include any tags, certificates, cards, or accessories supplied with the product
  • Not show signs of damage, wear, scratches, or alteration

Items returned after the 30-day return period may not be accepted.

2. Jewelry-Specific Return Conditions

Because jewelry is a personal-use product, returned items must be in new and resalable condition.

We may reject a return if the item has:

  • Been worn
  • Scratches, dents, or visible damage
  • Missing stones or components
  • Signs of perfume, cosmetics, lotion, or other residue
  • Been resized, engraved, altered, repaired, or modified
  • Missing original packaging or accessories

For hygiene and safety reasons, certain earrings or other personal-use jewelry may not be eligible for return once opened or worn, where permitted by applicable law.

3. Personalized or Customized Jewelry

Personalized, engraved, custom-made, or specially modified jewelry may be non-returnable, except where the item is defective, damaged, incorrectly made, or applicable law provides otherwise.

Please review the product description carefully before purchasing customized items.

4. How to Request a Return

Before sending an item back, please contact us at:

Email: hello@urbanstore.com

Please include:

  • Order number
  • Name used for the order
  • Item you wish to return
  • Reason for the return
  • Photographs, if the item is damaged or defective

We will provide return instructions after reviewing your request.

Please do not send a return to an address without receiving return instructions from us.

5. Return Shipping

For a standard change-of-mind return, the customer may be responsible for the cost of return shipping unless otherwise stated at checkout or required by applicable law.

If an item is:

  • Defective
  • Damaged during delivery
  • Incorrectly shipped

we will review the issue and may provide an appropriate resolution, which may include replacement, exchange, or refund.

6. Damaged or Incorrect Items

Please contact us as soon as possible if your package arrives damaged or you receive the wrong product.

We may request photographs of:

  • The jewelry
  • Product packaging
  • Shipping package
  • Shipping label

This information helps us investigate the issue with our fulfillment or delivery partners.

7. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect an eligible returned item, we will notify you whether the return has been approved.

If approved, the refund will generally be issued to the original payment method.

Refund processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

Shipping charges may not be refundable unless the return is due to an error, defect, damage, or another circumstance where a refund is required by applicable law.

8. Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an eligible item for another product, please contact us before returning the item.

Exchanges are subject to product availability.

If the requested replacement is unavailable, we may offer a refund or another suitable option.

9. Sale and Promotional Items

Sale or promotional items may be subject to different return conditions.

Any special return restrictions will be clearly communicated on the relevant product page or at checkout.

Nothing in this section limits any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

10. Lost or Undelivered Returns

Customers are responsible for ensuring that returned packages are properly packaged and shipped to the address provided by Urban Store Jewelry.

We recommend using a shipping service that provides tracking for returned jewelry.

We may not be able to process a refund for a return that does not reach us.

11. Consumer Rights

This policy does not affect any mandatory consumer rights available under applicable law.

Customers in the United States, United Kingdom, and other jurisdictions may have statutory rights that apply regardless of the terms of this policy.

Where applicable law provides a greater right than this policy, the applicable law will take precedence.

12. Changes to This Policy

Urban Store Jewelry may update this Return & Refund Policy from time to time.

The latest version will be posted on this page with the updated Last Updated date.

13. Contact Us

For return, exchange, or refund questions, please contact:

Urban Store Jewelry
Email: hello@urbanstore.com
