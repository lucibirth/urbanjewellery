Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Urban Store Jewelry, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you interact with our website, including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Shipping and billing information, when applicable
  • Contact information
  • Messages or enquiries submitted through our contact form
  • Information you provide when requesting customer support

We may also automatically collect limited technical information, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate geographic location
  • Website usage and interaction information

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to customer enquiries
  • Provide customer support
  • Process and manage orders, where applicable
  • Communicate about orders, returns, refunds, or other customer-service matters
  • Improve our website, products, and services
  • Maintain website security and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of our website and services without an appropriate legal basis or your consent where required.

3. Payment Information

If online payments are available, payment transactions may be processed by third-party payment providers.

Urban Store Jewelry does not need to store your complete payment card details on its own servers. Payment information may be handled directly by the relevant payment provider according to its privacy policy and security practices.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep the website functioning properly
  • Remember preferences
  • Understand website traffic
  • Improve website performance
  • Support security and fraud prevention

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Information Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our website and provide services, including:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Payment processors
  • Shipping and delivery providers
  • Website analytics providers
  • Customer-support services
  • Security and fraud-prevention providers

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable contractual or legal requirements.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including fulfilling transactions, providing customer support, resolving disputes, maintaining business records, and meeting legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we may securely delete or anonymize it where appropriate.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, which may include:

  • Requesting access to personal information we hold about you
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information
  • Requesting deletion of certain information
  • Requesting restriction of certain processing
  • Objecting to certain uses of your information
  • Requesting a copy of certain personal information
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent

To make a privacy-related request, please contact Urban Store Jewelry using the contact information provided on our website.

9. United States Customers

Privacy rights may vary depending on the state in which you reside. Where applicable, Urban Store Jewelry will handle privacy requests in accordance with relevant state privacy laws.

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration.

10. United Kingdom Customers

For customers in the United Kingdom, we aim to process personal information in accordance with applicable UK data-protection requirements.

Where required, we will provide appropriate information regarding the legal basis for processing personal information and applicable individual rights.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

12. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to make a privacy-related request, please contact:
